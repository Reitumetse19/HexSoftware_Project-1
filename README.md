# **Student Depression Dataset**

A comprehensive dataset aimed at analyzing and understanding depression levels among students, incorporating various demographic, academic, lifestyle, and psychological factors.

### **Dataset Overview**

**Format**: CSV

**Total Records**: e.g., 1,000 students

### **Features**:

- **Demographics**: Age, Gender, City

- **Academic Metrics**: CGPA, Academic Pressure, Study Satisfaction

- **Lifestyle Factors**: Sleep Duration, Work/Study Hours, Dietary Habits

- **Psychological Indicators**: Work Pressure, Job Satisfaction, Depression Status

### **Key Findings**

- **Academic Pressure**: Positively correlated with depression levels.

- **Work/Study Hours**: Higher hours associate with increased depression.

- **Age**: Younger students tend to report higher depression levels.

- **Study Satisfaction**: Negatively correlated with depression; higher satisfaction links to lower depression levels.
